DocumentStore stores your PDFs and photos locally on your device and backs
them up to a "DocumentStore" folder in your own Google Drive.

- We request access to your Google account name, email address, and files
  that this app itself creates in your Drive (drive.file scope).
- Files never leave your device and your own Drive. We have no server and
  collect no analytics.
- To revoke access: Sign out in the app, or visit
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove DocumentStore.
